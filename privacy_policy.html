Official Privacy Policy

Mr. Board

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 • Last Updated: November 7, 2026

Developed by Mr. HU Apps

🔒 Zero Data Collection Guarantee: Mr. Board is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do not collect, monitor, store, or transmit your keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, photos, or device files to any server. All processing runs 100% on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Mr. HU Apps ("we", "our", or "developer") handles privacy and data protection within the Mr. Board Android application (package: com.mrhuapp.mrboard). We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting it in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and international data protection regulations.

2. Keyboard Input & Typing Privacy

As a native Input Method Service (IME), Mr. Board prioritizes keystroke privacy above all else:

  • No Keystroke Logging: What you type into Mr. Board never leaves your device. We do not operate logging databases, remote cloud servers, or keyloggers.
  • Sensitive Field Protection: When you enter passwords, PINs, OTP codes, or financial credentials, Mr. Board automatically detects the secure input flag and disables word suggestions, clipboard caching, and cheat-code detection.
  • Local Prediction Dictionaries: All multi-language bigram suggestions and vocabulary dictionaries run strictly on-device using pre-bundled lexical mappings.
  • Local Clipboard Manager: Snippets copied to the internal clipboard are stored in local sandbox memory and can be cleared or deleted by you at any time.

3. Mr. Mouse Precision Pad & Accessibility Service

Mr. Board provides an optional software trackpad and floating overlay called Mr. Mouse that requires the Android Accessibility API (AccessibilityService) to function:

  • Single Purpose: The Accessibility Service is used exclusively to simulate user-commanded touch actions (taps, double taps, right-click long presses, drag-and-drop, and scroll gestures) when you interact with the software mouse pad.
  • Zero Surveillance: Mr. Mouse strictly does NOT inspect window contents, does NOT read on-screen text, does NOT record keystrokes, and does NOT monitor your activities in other applications.
  • Full User Control: You can enable or disable the Accessibility Engine at any time in Android System Settings.

4. Steganography Studio & Media Privacy

The Steganography Studio allows users to embed encrypted messages into images using 24-bit RGB Least Significant Bit (LSB) encoding with AES-GCM 256-bit encryption:

  • On-Device Encryption: All encoding and decoding happens locally on your device CPU. Your photos and secret keys are never uploaded to the cloud.
  • Scoped Storage: Stealth images are saved directly to your device public pictures directory (Pictures/MrBoardStego).
  • Secure Original Photo Deletion: If you choose to delete the unencoded original photo after embedding, Mr. Board uses the official Android MediaStore.createDeleteRequest system API, which explicitly asks for your operating system confirmation before deleting the file.

5. Digital Signature Studio

The Digital Signature Studio enables drawing and exporting transparent vector signatures and date stamps:

  • Signatures and verification timestamps are rendered locally on your device screen.
  • Exported PNG files are stored in your device storage (Pictures/MrBoardSignatures) for your personal use. They are never transmitted externally.

6. 100% Ad-Free & Zero Third-Party Trackers

Mr. Board is completely ad-free and tracking-free:

  • We do NOT integrate advertising networks (no Unity Ads, Google AdMob, or banner networks).
  • We do NOT embed behavioral analytics, location trackers, or profiling SDKs.
  • No third-party SDKs have access to your device data through this application.

7. Device Permissions & Justifications

Mr. Board requests only the minimal permissions required to provide its core utility features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides haptic feedback when pressing keyboard keys or trackpad buttons.
  • android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Allows the floating Mr. Mouse trackpad overlay to appear over other applications when triggered by the user.
  • android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Dispatches touch gestures commanding cursor clicks, scrolls, and drag-and-drop.
  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Required on Android 13+ solely when you request Steganography Studio to identify and prompt deletion of an original unencrypted photo.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Enables Direct Boot support so the keyboard is ready immediately when your device starts.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Mr. Board does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The application does not contain behavioral profiling, age-gated advertisements, or social chat channels.

9. Security of Your Data

Because we do not store, collect, or transmit your personal data, your security is tied directly to your physical device. We utilize industry-standard AES-256 GCM encryption algorithms for protected tools (Steganography Studio and Password Generator), ensuring your encrypted data remains mathematically secure.
